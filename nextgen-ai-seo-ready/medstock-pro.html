The operating problem

Pharmacy records become difficult when stock, sales and credit live in separate places.

Small and growing pharmacies often manage medicine inventory, handwritten customer credit, daily expenses and sales reporting as separate tasks. That fragmentation makes it harder to understand what is available, what is expiring, which bills remain unpaid and where money moved during the day.

MedStock Pro creates one connected record. A completed bill changes stock, an Udhar bill retains its original invoice association, every installment reduces the remaining balance, and authorized expenses become visible alongside operational activity.

Core workflows

Pharmacy operations without disconnected records.

Each capability is designed as part of the same operating flow rather than as an isolated feature.

Medicine inventory

Designed for operational reality

Useful when connectivity, speed and accountability all matter.

Offline-ready by design

Core pharmacy records remain available on the device for day-to-day continuity. When persistent synchronization is configured and the internet is available, account and pharmacy data can be backed up for restoration.

Financial records stay separate and traceable

All Bills, the Udhar Ledger, Expenses and Reports remain distinct operating areas. This helps the owner move from an individual transaction to the relevant financial view without collapsing everything into one generic report.

Medical information remains reviewable

The extraction workflow is an operational aid—not a dispensing, diagnosis or clinical decision system. Qualified users remain responsible for checking medicine details.

Questions

MedStock Pro FAQ

Clear answers about the current product and how it is intended to be used.

What does MedStock Pro manage?

It connects pharmacy inventory, batches, expiry dates, POS billing, customer Udhar, installment payments, expenses, vendors, reports and audit history.

Can it work without a continuous internet connection?

The application is offline-ready for core local workflows. Persistent cloud synchronization requires an internet connection when backing up or restoring data.

Does it support partial Udhar payments?

Yes. A bill can retain an opening payment, remaining balance, due date and multiple later installments with complete payment history.

Can staff and owners use the same pharmacy record?

Yes. Staff accounts can be linked to an owner pharmacy. Role controls reserve selected financial and administrative actions for the owner.

Is medicine extraction a medical AI result?

No. OCR-assisted extraction is an input workflow and every field must be checked by a qualified user. It must not be used for diagnosis, dispensing or clinical decisions.
